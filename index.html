Australia's
Mammal Story

How isolation shaped a unique
community of mammals

Australia is home to one of the world's most distinctive mammal communities.

Cut off from other landmasses, Australia's native species evolved in deep geographic solitude. This isolation shaped unique biological adaptations, distinct feeding behaviors, and localized survival strategies.

🐨

Fun Fact

Australia has more marsupial species than any other continent!

01

A MAMMAL COMMUNITY LIKE NO OTHER

Australia's mammalian fauna is defined by extreme evolutionary divergence. While placental mammals dominate most global landmasses, tens of millions of years in isolation allowed marsupials and monotremes to flourish in ecological niches elsewhere held by placentals.
